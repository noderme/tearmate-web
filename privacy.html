Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following personal data:

  • Phone number — for account verification and authentication via one-time SMS codes.
  • Date of birth — to verify that you are 18 years of age or older before creating an account.
  • Name, profile photo, and bio — voluntarily provided by you and displayed to other users.
  • Device location (GPS coordinates) — collected while the app is in use to show you nearby helpers or seekers. Not stored permanently after a session ends.
  • Chat messages — messages sent between users during a session, stored in our secure database.
  • Session history, ratings, and reviews — to build trust and safety within the community.
  • Safety event flags — if our automated safety system detects a potential crisis signal in a message, a timestamped flag is recorded against the session for safeguarding purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your phone number is used solely for identity verification. Your date of birth is used once to confirm you are 18 or older; it is stored securely and not shared with other users. Your location is used in real-time to match you with nearby helpers or seekers. Your profile information is displayed to other users to facilitate sessions. Chat messages are stored to allow the conversation to function and are subject to automated safety screening (see Section 3). Session history and ratings improve platform safety. Safety event flags are used only for safeguarding purposes and are not used for advertising or profiling.

3. Automated Safety Screening

To protect user safety and comply with applicable laws including the UK Online Safety Act 2023, TearMate operates an automated crisis detection system. This system analyses the text content of chat messages to identify signals that a user may be in immediate distress or danger. When such a signal is detected, both users in the session are shown a screen with emergency helpline numbers.

This processing is carried out by TearMate and uses Anthropic PBC ("Anthropic") as a data processor. Message content is transmitted to Anthropic's API solely for crisis classification and is not used for any other purpose. Anthropic's privacy policy governs their handling of data. The lawful basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in user safety and our legal obligations under applicable online safety legislation.

4. Information Sharing and Third-Party Processors

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as necessary to operate the platform:

  • Google LLC (Firebase) — authentication, database, push notifications. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Google LLC (Maps) — location display within the app.
  • Anthropic PBC — automated safety screening of chat messages (see Section 3). Messages are processed for classification only and are not retained by Anthropic beyond their standard data handling practices.
  • Apple Inc. — payment processing for in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies.

All third-party processors are required to handle data in accordance with applicable data protection law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Account profile (name, photo, bio, phone number, date of birth) — retained while your account is active. Deleted upon account deletion.
  • Location data — not stored after a session ends.
  • Chat messages — retained for 12 months from the date of the session, then permanently deleted.
  • Session records and ratings — retained for 3 years for trust and safety purposes.
  • Safety event flags — retained for 90 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Incident reports submitted by users — retained for 90 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Transaction records — retained for a minimum of 7 years as required by financial regulations.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time via the app or by contacting privacy@tearmate.online.

6. Children's Privacy — 18+ Only

TearMate is strictly for users aged 18 and over. We collect your date of birth during registration to verify your age. Users who cannot confirm they are 18 or older cannot create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately at privacy@tearmate.online and we will delete the account without delay.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (TLS), Firebase security rules to restrict data access, and phone number verification to prevent unauthorised account creation. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights (UK GDPR and US)

If you are in the United Kingdom or European Economic Area, you have rights under UK GDPR / GDPR including: the right to access your data, the right to correct inaccurate data, the right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"), the right to restrict or object to processing, and the right to data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States via Google Firebase and Anthropic infrastructure. Such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards in accordance with UK GDPR.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@tearmate.online.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your data rights, contact us at privacy@tearmate.online.
