No Payment Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

Withly is completely free

There are no session fees, subscription charges, in-app purchases, or any other payments on Withly. Helpers are volunteers. Seekers pay nothing. There is nothing to refund.

No money between users

Helpers must not request payment from Seekers at any point — before, during, or after a session. Seekers must not offer payment to Helpers. Any exchange of money between users is a violation of our Terms of Service and will result in immediate account removal.

Questions

If you have any questions, contact us at support@withly.app.
